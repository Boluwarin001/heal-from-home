Why Heal From Home

Health starts with what we can practise every day.

Heal From Home aims to empower individuals and families with practical knowledge of herbal healing and organic food cultivation. The programme combines healthy living, natural approaches, small-space cultivation and entrepreneurship into one structured learning pathway.

01

Healthy living

Learn healthy living and natural approaches through food and herbs.

02

Grow at home

Develop skills to grow organic foods and medicinal herbs in small or limited spaces.

03

Create income

Explore ways to monetise farming knowledge and products.

04

Build sustainably

Follow a structured pathway from learning and practical work to online training.

The learning journey

Three modules. One practical pathway.

The programme moves from nutrition and natural wellness into cultivation, then into the business skills needed to turn agricultural knowledge into products and opportunities.

Meet the people

A multidisciplinary team behind the programme.

General Overseer

Pastor Darasimi Gomba Oyor

A Christian counsellor, author, minister and advocate for personal transformation, wholesome living and Kingdom-centred development. She is the founder of The Garden, a counselling and mentorship platform, and Ezer Intensive Academy.

She is a certified chaplain, a professional Christian counselor, and a member of the American Association of Christian Counselors. Darasimi is also an author and spoken-word minister.

Programme Creator & Director

Nicolle Joy

Inspired by her own experience of overcoming debilitating health challenges through prayer, nutrition and lifestyle changes, Nicole designed Heal From Home to help others take greater control of health and wellbeing.

With over 10 years of experience as a successful businesswoman, she also brings entrepreneurship into the programme, helping families become healthier, more self-sufficient and economically empowered from home.

Facilitator · Agriculture

Afuhbom Daniel

An agricultural expert, agro-engineer and founder of Danny's Garden of Knowledge, focused on practical agricultural knowledge, natural health systems and sustainable living.

His interests include organic soil health, sustainable farming techniques and teaching young people practical approaches to agriculture.

Agri Business Expert · Facilitator

Adeyinka Adetunji

An educator, agribusiness professional and curriculum developer with a passion for empowering learners through practical, engaging and transformative education.

He is the founder of Best Scholars Edu Consult and is actively involved in ADFORA AGRIBUSINESS, with experience in agribusiness management, livestock production and sustainable agriculture.

Facilitator · Nutrition

Oluwakemi Taiwo

A Public Health Nutritionist, Researcher and Health Data Analyst passionate about community-based initiatives and public health nutrition, with a strong interest in preventive healthcare and evidence-based interventions.
